Method

A proven methodology.

Four steps, in order. Each one narrows the map from every solution we can find to a verified profile.

  1. 01

    Identification and mapping

    National research across several channels identifies the Made in Italy tech solutions.

  2. 02

    Category classification

    A conceptual framework divides the mapped landscape into specific categories, so the map stays comparable year after year.

  3. 03

    Desk analysis

    Official channels, including chamber-of-commerce data, collect company information by category.

  4. 04

    On-field analysis

    Each company completes a questionnaire on its own solutions, which becomes an individual, verified profile.

Methodology: ITL 2026, p. 10.
